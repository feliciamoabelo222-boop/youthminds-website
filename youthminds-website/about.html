About Young & Heard

We're a non-profit built by psychologists, school counsellors, and young people who believe mental health support should meet teenagers where they actually are.

Our Story

Young & Heard Wellness Hub started after a group of school counsellors noticed the same gap again and again: mental health resources written for adults, handed to teenagers, and expected to work. We built something different — peer-led, youth-first, and honest about what growing up actually feels like.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To give young people the tools, language, and community to understand and manage their mental health early in life.

Vision: A generation of young people who feel confident asking for help without shame.

Our Team

Our team includes registered counsellors, youth workers, and trained peer facilitators who lead our support circles.
