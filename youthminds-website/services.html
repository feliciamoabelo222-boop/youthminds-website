Our Services

Every service we offer is designed for young people first — no jargon, no judgement.

Peer Support Circles

Small, confidential groups of 6–8 young people, guided by a trained facilitator. Circles meet weekly and focus on anxiety, depression, bullying, and abuse recovery in a way that feels like talking to a friend, not a lecture.

School Programmes

We partner with schools to run mental health workshops, assembly talks, and teacher training sessions that help build a culture where students feel safe to speak up.

Referral Counselling

For young people who need one-on-one support, we refer to a network of counsellors experienced in working with teenagers and young adults.
